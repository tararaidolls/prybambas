Frequently Asked Questions

What is Prybambas?

A free STEM hub with three games: What If (logic), Inventor Lab (creative thinking), and AI Coach (languages).

Is Prybambas free?

Yes. Completely free. No login or subscription.

What age is Prybambas for?

Ages 5 to 14. Works for families and classrooms.

What languages does Prybambas support?

English, Ukrainian, Polish, Spanish, German, French, Romanian, Italian, Portuguese, Swedish, and more.

What is the What If game?

A cause-and-effect logic game: explore scenarios, build logic chains, solve deduction puzzles.

What is Inventor Lab?

Creative problem-solving inspired by TRIZ: invention tools, daily challenges, design missions.
