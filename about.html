About Prybambas

Prybambas is a free STEM education hub for curious kids ages 5–14. No login required.

Three learning spaces

  • What If? — logic and cause-and-effect game. Modes: single round, logic chain, deduction.
  • Inventor Lab — TRIZ-inspired creative thinking: tools, 30-day calendar, challenges, missions.
  • AI Coach — language practice companion.

Who it's for

Children ages 5–14, parents, homeschoolers, teachers, and multilingual families.

Languages

English, Ukrainian, Polish, Spanish, German, French, Romanian, Italian, Portuguese, Swedish, and more.
