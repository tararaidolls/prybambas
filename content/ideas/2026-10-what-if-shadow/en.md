Today's question from the What If? game:

**Your shadow decided to live its own life and no longer copies your moves.**

Ask your child to continue the chain:
1. What would people notice first?
2. What would change on the street, at school, in sports?
3. What new job would appear because of it?

> Tip: don't rush the answer. Ask "why?" after every step.
